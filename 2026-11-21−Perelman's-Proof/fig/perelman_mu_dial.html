The μ dial: a torus closes into a disc and comes out turned over

-4

The dial μ moves only through whole numbers, from −4 to +4 and back; nothing is interpolated between them. Below zero (gold) the surface is a torus whose hole has radius √(−μ). At μ = 0 (red) the hole closes into a disc. Above zero (blue) the centre opens the other way, to a thickness of 2√μ. One surface formula covers all nine steps, and nothing is cut anywhere along the way. Drag to turn it.

Read in the radius r, the passage through zero is an end that would need one surgery. Read in μ = ±r², it is one more integer step.
