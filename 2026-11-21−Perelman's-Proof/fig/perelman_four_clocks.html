Same integers, four different clocks

The Fibonacci integers 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, … are the same whatever clock you read them on. Read on clocks of 11, 13, 23 and 29 hours, they produce four different ledger staircases. The ticks on the bottom axis mark where each clock completes a period. In every case m × F = S holds exactly, where F is the number of firings per period and S the sum of residues.

So the number of firings is not a property of the sequence. It belongs to the clock you read it on.
