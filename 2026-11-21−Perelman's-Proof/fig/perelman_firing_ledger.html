Fibonacci by addition only, read on a 13-hour clock

Each step adds the two previous residues. Both are below 13, so the sum is below 26 and one subtraction of 13 is always enough. Whether it happened is a clean 0 or 1: a firing. The ledger on the right is the running total of firings. It never decreases and climbs one whole step at a time.

After one full turn (28 steps) the ledger reads 12, and the residues add up to 156 = 13 × 12. Adding the step rn+2 = rn+1 + rn − 13 fn over a period gives S = S + S − 13F, so 13 × F = S. Everything on this page is integer arithmetic.
